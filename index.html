Six seasons in bangladesh

Six seasons

Summer

Summer begins from the third week of April and continues up to the end of June. It is the hottest season of the year. The sun sometimes seems to scorch the earth. So, people generally keep indoors at noon as far as practicable. Most of the tanks and ponds dry up.

Monsoon

Monsoon in Bangladesh extends from the end of June to the end of August. These are the months of rain. Tanks and ponds, and canals get filled up. Sometimes, the rain is so heavy as to cause floods, damaging crops and properties.

Autum

Autumn in Bangladesh comprises the months of September and October. The sky gets clear, and we often enjoy the beauty of moonlight in all its sweetness. Paddy is harvested in this season.

Late-Autum

Late-Autumn: After autumn comes late autumn. ‘Kartik’ and ‘Agrayhoyan’ make this season. In this season, dew drops begin to fall on leaves. Farmers look happy because they are busy with reaping paddy and other crops.

Winter

Spring

Spring: Last of all, there comes spring, which consists of the Bengali months ‘Falgun’ and ‘Chaitra’. Spring is called the king of all seasons. Nature looks beautiful. Trees get new leaves and flowers. A gentle breeze blows. Bees hum, and the cuckoo sings in joy.
